#+title: Education, health and care plans
#+subtitle: Reporting year 2026

* Data guidance

Description of the data sets included in this release, including
information on data sources, coverage, quality and any data
conventions used.

* Description

This document describes the data included in the Education, health and
care (EHC) plans National Statistics release’s underlying data
files. This data is released under the terms of the Open Government
License and is intended to meet at least 3 stars for Open Data. The
methodology document should be referenced alongside this data. It
provides information on the data sources, their coverage and quality
as well as explaining methodology used in producing the data.

* Coverage

This release provides information on various stages of the EHC plan process including:

 - The number of active EHC plans at January of the calendar year
 - The number of new EHC plans issued during previous calendar year
 - The number of requests for an EHC needs assessment received during
   the previous calendar year
 - The number of EHC needs assessments completed during in the
   previous calendar year
 - The type of primary need named in the EHC plan, for both active EHC
   plans at January and new plans issued during the previous calendar
   year
 - The number of plans issued with the statutory timeframe during the
   previous calendar year
 - The number of EHC plans where a personal budget is in place
 - The number of work based placements - this includes supported
   internships, apprenticeships and traineeships.
 - The number of residential placements
 - The number of annual reviews and phase transfers
 - The number of plans which ceased during the the previous calendar
   year

* Data sources

In 2023 the source of data on EHC plans changed. Prior to 2023 the
data collection was local authority level  with aggregated data being
collected for a range of indicators and outcomes from each local
authority.

In 2023 the collection changed to individual level. The local
authority returns information on:

 - Each request for an EHC needs assessments and its outcome
 - Each EHC needs assessment carried out and its outcome
 - The content of all plans, this includes the type of special
   educational need, the setting named in plan, the setting attended,
   the date the plan started or ceased, annual reviews which have
   taken place and features of the plan such as personal budgets and
   residential placements.
 - Personal details of the child or young person with a request,
   assessment or plan in place

This individual level data is then aggregated to calculate national,
regional and local authority totals.

This change in data source has necessitated a change in how figures
are presented and how the time series of data is shown. Some data
items collected in the pre-2023 aggregate collection are no longer
available while the individual data now collected allows a much richer
and increased range of detailed analysis to be produced. As such there
has been a break in the time series. In 2014, changes to the [[https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/send-code-of-practice-0-to-25][SEN code
of Practice]] replaced statements of special educational needs (SEN) and
Learning Difficulty Assessments (LDAs) with Education, Health and Care
(EHC) plans. Those with a statement of SEN were converted to EHC
plans. This process was completed by 2018. Therefore the time series
included in this publication begins at 2019. Data for the years prior
to 2019 remains available.   The [[https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/education-health-and-care-plans/2024][2024 edition of this statistical
release]] includes information on EHC plans prior to 2019 plus
information on statements of SEN for each year from 2010. Where
previous data exists figures have been given to provide a time series
of covering each of the 7 years from 2018/19.
